Why ACDM exists

Agentic AI changes the consumer of enterprise data.

Traditional schemas and catalogs describe what data looks like, but they do not always tell an agent whether data is fresh enough, traceable enough, trusted enough, affordable enough, or safe enough for a specific class of action. ACDM makes those expectations explicit before runtime.

Methodology

The five agent-facing properties.

Trust

Declares confidence in the entity and how conservative downstream use should be.

Freshness

Sets maximum data age expectations so stale data does not silently drive agent decisions.

Provenance

Requires traceability to origins, transformations, ownership, and audit context.

Cost

Makes operational or monetary consumption costs visible before automated workflows run.

Risk

Classifies sensitivity and action risk so unsafe use is blocked or constrained.

Reference implementation

Validate ACDM contracts before agents consume the data.

The acdm-validator project turns the methodology into governance-as-code checks for entity contracts, composition rules, permission ceilings, maturity stages, and audit records.
